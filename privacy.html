Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy explains how FIFA Bros handles information when you use the iOS app. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to rsjobalia@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account information: a provider account identifier, email address, and, when supplied by Apple or Google, your display name and profile image URL.
  • Squad and match information: squad names and join codes; player names, emoji and colours; match scores, dates, teams, notes and badge progress.
  • Optional proof photos: an image you choose from your photo library to attach to a match.
  • Service information: basic security and operational records that our hosting and authentication providers process to deliver and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, sync your scorekeeping data, let members collaborate in a shared squad, restore data on your devices, provide support, prevent abuse and maintain the service.

Photos

Adding a proof photo is optional. The app accesses only the image you select, reduces its size, keeps a local copy and uploads it to a private storage bucket so members of that squad can view it. Proof photos are not public and are not used for advertising or tracking.

Sharing and service providers

Members of a squad can see and edit the squad's shared players, matches and proof photos. We use the following providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase for authentication, database syncing, server functions and private file storage.
  • Apple when you choose Sign in with Apple.
  • Google when you choose Sign in with Google.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information, show targeted advertising, or use third-party advertising trackers.

Storage and security

Synced data is hosted in Supabase's India region. The app uses authentication and row-level access rules intended to restrict data to signed-in users and members of the relevant squad. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and game data while your account is active or as reasonably needed to operate the service, resolve security issues and meet legal obligations. You can request deletion directly in the app at Settings → Delete Account. Deleting an account removes the account and associated data controlled by that account; information that another squad member controls may remain with that shared squad. You can also email us for help with a deletion request.

Your choices

You may decline to add proof photos, leave shared squads where the app provides that option, export supported data, sign out, or delete your account. Your device settings control photo-library permission.

Children

FIFA Bros is not directed to children below the minimum age required to consent to online services in their country. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

International processing

Apple, Google and their subprocessors may process information in countries other than yours. Their privacy policies describe their processing and safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will remain posted here with its updated date.
