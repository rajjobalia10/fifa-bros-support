Roundel support

For help with Roundel, report a problem or make a privacy request, email raj@rivesa.ai.

Report a problem

Include the app version, your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead.

A screenshot can help. Remove personal information before sharing it. Please do not send passwords, payment details, private photos, health information or location history.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address for questions about privacy or information you have shared with support. Describe the request without attaching sensitive app data.
