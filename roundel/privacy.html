Roundel privacy policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Publisher: Raj Jobalia

Information in the app

Your recorded trip routes, stops, notes, preferences and optional step counts and references to matched photos are stored on your device. Trip history is not uploaded to the developer.

Location and Apple Maps

You start and stop trip recording. Location permission is needed to record a route; background recording uses the additional location permission you choose to grant. Apple Maps, place search and geocoding can send map, search and location information to Apple under Apple’s privacy practices. You can change location access in iOS Settings.

Optional photos and step counts

If you enable photo matching and grant Photos access, Roundel uses the permitted photos’ timestamps and, when available, location metadata to associate photos with trips. You can grant limited or full library access and change it in iOS Settings. The app keeps local photo-library references; Apple’s Photos services may download images from iCloud Photos to display them. If you enable step counting and grant Motion & Fitness access, Roundel reads step counts for trip periods using Apple’s pedometer service. It does not read Apple Health. These features are optional.

Developer services

This version does not require an online account and has no configured developer-operated backend, advertising or analytics service. The developer does not receive the contents of your local app library through normal app use.

Storage, sharing and deletion

Device backups may include app data according to your iOS settings. When you choose to share content, the destination you select has its own privacy practices. Use the app’s available deletion controls to remove records. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data; exported copies, original items in Photos and backups must be managed separately.

Support and this website

If you email support, Raj Jobalia receives your email address and the information you include and uses it to respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive app data. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; its host may process technical request information to serve and protect the website. Website requests are separate from the data stored in the app.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests about information you shared with support, contact raj@rivesa.ai. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
