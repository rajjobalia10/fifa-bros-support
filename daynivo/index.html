Tally support

For help with Tally, to report a problem or to make a privacy request, email raj@rivesa.ai.

Report a problem

Include the app version (Settings → About), your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps; remove personal information first.

Your account and data

  • Sign in or out: Profile → Account.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and synced data.
  • Export a copy: Settings → Export my data.
  • Reminders: Settings → Notifications, or iOS Settings → Tally → Notifications.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address for any privacy question or request.
