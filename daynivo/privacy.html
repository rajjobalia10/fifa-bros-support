Tally privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Publisher: Raj Jobalia · Applies to the iOS app Tally (bundle id com.pkd.daynivo, formerly Daynivo)

The short version

  • Tally works without an account. Signed out, everything you enter stays on your iPhone.
  • If you choose to sign in with Apple, your plan is synced to your account so it follows you to a new phone.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.
  • You can delete your account, and everything synced with it, from inside the app at any time.

Information stored on your device

Your routines and tasks, notes, completions and streaks, categories, settings, and the answers you give during setup (your name, goal, focus areas, your "why" line, the days a week you commit to, reminder time, and the inputs you choose in the compounding calculator) are saved on your iPhone. Device backups may include this data according to your iOS settings.

Optional account (Sign in with Apple)

If you sign in, we receive from Apple a stable user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (which may be an Apple private-relay address). We store these together with your synced plan: tasks, completions, categories and the setup answers listed above. This data is linked to your account and used only to provide sync. It is stored with our database and authentication provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf.

Permissions

Tally asks for one permission: notifications, used only for the reminders you switch on (a daily reminder at the time you pick, and optional weekly and refill reminders). These are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings. Tally does not use your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or Apple Health.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising or any other purpose, and we do not track you across apps or websites. When you use the share sheet (for example to export your data or share a streak receipt), the destination you pick has its own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Signed-in data is kept while your account exists. In the app, Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account and every synced record from our servers and from the phone. Settings → Clear local data removes data from the device, and Settings → Export my data gives you a copy. Deleting the app removes its on-device data.

Children

Tally is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Support and this website

If you email support, Raj Jobalia receives your email address and what you include, and uses it only to respond. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information to serve and protect the site.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact raj@rivesa.ai. If our data practices change we will update this page and its effective date.
