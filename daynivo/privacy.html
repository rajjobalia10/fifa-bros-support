Daynivo privacy policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Publisher: Raj Jobalia

Information in the app

Tasks, schedules, task completion, preferences and profile information you enter are stored on your device. The planning assistant generates local responses and does not send your questions to an AI service. This version does not read Apple Health or measure health signals.

Permissions and platform services

Optional reminders use iOS local notifications. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Developer services

This version does not require an online account and has no configured developer-operated backend, advertising or analytics service. The developer does not receive the contents of your local app library through normal app use.

Storage, sharing and deletion

Device backups may include app data according to your iOS settings. When you choose to share content, the destination you select has its own privacy practices. Use the app’s available deletion controls to remove records. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data; exported copies, original items in Photos and backups must be managed separately.

Support and this website

If you email support, Raj Jobalia receives your email address and the information you include and uses it to respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive app data. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; its host may process technical request information to serve and protect the website. Website requests are separate from the data stored in the app.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests about information you shared with support, contact raj@rivesa.ai. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
