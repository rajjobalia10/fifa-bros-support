Habit Kingdom support

For help with Habit Kingdom, to report a problem or to make a privacy request, email raj@rivesa.ai.

Report a problem

Include the app version (the bottom of the Settings tab), your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps; remove personal information first.

How it works

  • Add a habit: Habits → + . Name any habit you like, or start from an idea.
  • Keep a habit: tap it on the Habits tab. Each habit you keep earns gold and stone and builds the next piece of your kingdom.
  • Archive or restore a habit: hold a habit on Habits to archive it. Settings → Archived habits → Restore brings it back with its history.
  • Progress: the Progress tab shows your commitment and the last 26 weeks. Tap a day to see it.

Your account and data

  • Use Habit Kingdom without an account: choose Continue as guest. Everything stays on your iPhone.
  • Sign in or out: Settings → Continue with Apple, or Settings → Sign out.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete your account. This permanently removes your account and your kingdom from our servers and your iPhone.
  • Erase data as a guest: Settings → Delete your account → Delete everything.
  • Reminders: Settings → Daily reminder, or iOS Settings → Habit Kingdom → Notifications.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address for any privacy question or request.
