Habit Kingdom privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Publisher: Raj Jobalia · Applies to the iOS app Habit Kingdom (bundle id com.pkd.habitkingdom)

The short version

  • Habit Kingdom works without an account. As a guest, everything you enter stays on your iPhone.
  • If you choose to sign in with Apple, your kingdom is synced to your account so it follows you to a new phone.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.
  • You can delete your account, and everything synced with it, from inside the app at any time.

Information stored on your device

Your name, your reason for building habits, your habits (their names and icons), the days you kept them, your kingdom (gold, stone and buildings), notes you write for a day, your avatar choice (and, if you pick one, your avatar photo), the time of your daily reminder and your appearance setting are saved on your iPhone. Notes and avatar photos never leave your iPhone. Your kingdom and notes are excluded from device backups, so deleting the app removes them; a signed-in kingdom comes back when you sign in again.

Optional account (Sign in with Apple)

If you sign in, we receive from Apple a stable user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (which may be an Apple private-relay address). We store these together with your synced kingdom: your habits, the days you kept them, your gold, stone and buildings. This data is linked to your account and used only to provide sync. It is stored with our database and authentication provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf.

Habit Kingdom uses the same sign-in service as other apps from this publisher. If you use another of them with the same Apple ID, that app shares the sign-in record, but never your Habit Kingdom data.

Permissions

Habit Kingdom asks for one permission: notifications, used only for the daily reminder at the time you pick. It is a local notification scheduled on your device, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings. If you choose your own avatar photo, you pick it with the iOS photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you select. Habit Kingdom does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or Apple Health.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising or any other purpose, and we do not track you across apps or websites. If you export your journal with the share sheet, the destination you pick has its own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Signed-in data is kept while your account exists. In the app, Settings → Delete your account permanently deletes your Habit Kingdom account and your synced kingdom from our servers and everything from the phone. If another app from this publisher still uses the same Apple ID, only the sign-in record that app needs is kept. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple with this app in iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple. Sign out keeps your kingdom in your account. As a guest, Settings → Delete your account removes everything from the device. Deleting the app removes its on-device data.

Children

Habit Kingdom is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Support and this website

If you email support, Raj Jobalia receives your email address and what you include, and uses it only to respond. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information to serve and protect the site.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact raj@rivesa.ai. If our data practices change we will update this page and its effective date.
