Stations support

Stations is a training app for fitness races that combine running with eight functional stations. For help, to report a problem or to make a privacy request, email raj@rivesa.ai. We reply within two working days.

Report a problem

Include the app version (bottom of the Profile tab), your iPhone model and iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Tap “Not now” at the account step and train as a guest. Everything stays on your iPhone.
  • Change my race date or division: Profile → Race. Plan-affecting changes offer “Rebuild plan from today”.
  • Move or skip a session: Plan → tap the session → Move, Swap or Skip.
  • Reminders: Profile → Notifications, or iOS Settings → Stations → Notifications.
  • Apple Health: optional and read-only. Turn it off in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Stations.
  • Delete my data: Profile → Delete account (signed in) or Erase data on this iPhone (guest).

Not affiliated

Stations is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by HYROX or Upsolut Sports. Station loads follow published race standards; always check your event’s current rulebook.
