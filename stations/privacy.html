Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Stations is an iPhone app for training for HYROX-style fitness races, made by Raj Jobalia. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and the choices you have. Last updated 25 November 2026.

The short version

Your training data stays on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive it, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data. Signing in with Apple is optional.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Profile: your name, race division and category, the sex your station loads are based on, experience level, optional birth year, race date and name, goal time, training days, session length, equipment, weak stations and units.
  • Training: your plan, the workouts you log (times, splits, effort, distance and any notes you write), and benchmarks and personal records.
  • Settings: sounds, haptics, countdown beeps and reminder preferences.

This is saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It's included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Signing in with Apple

Signing in is optional; you can train as a guest. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier for your account and, the first time only, the name and email you choose to share (Apple can give a private relay address instead of your real one). These are kept in your iPhone's Keychain so your training stays linked to your Apple account, and the name is used to fill in your profile. None of it is sent to us.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in the app, Stations asks to read your heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and VO2 max, including readings that devices such as Apple Watch or WHOOP save to Health. It uses them only on your iPhone, to show your recovery next to your training. It never writes to Health, never sends Health data anywhere, and never uses it for advertising. You can turn access off at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Notifications

Training reminders and race-week messages are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone from your plan. They never pass through a server. Stations only asks for permission when you turn reminders on, and you can turn them off in Profile or in Settings at any time.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, advertising identifiers or device fingerprints, and no Health data beyond the readings above. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Sharing

We don't share, sell or rent your data, because we don't receive it. If you choose to email support, we see only what you put in the email and use it only to help you.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays on your iPhone until you remove it. Guests can use Profile → Erase data on this iPhone. If you're signed in, Profile → Delete account erases all of your training from this iPhone, removes the sign-in from the Keychain and cancels reminders. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Stations in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account. Deleting the app deletes its data too.

Your rights

Because your data is on your device, you can see, change and delete it yourself in the app. If you have a question about your data or want to exercise a right you have under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, contact us below and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Stations is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age in your country, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we add features that send data off your iPhone, such as cloud backup, we'll update this policy first, tell you in the app, and ask before anything leaves your device. The date at the top shows the latest version.
